# Contributions

**Kleddamag** commissioned and directed the research, set its goals and
verification requirements, and maintains this repository.

**OpenAI Codex** developed the mathematical and computational continuation,
implemented and ran the searches and exact checks, and prepared the proof,
certificates and documentation.

The work builds on **Joshua Levy's squares project**, **Guzhou0806's R038
implementation**, and the earlier contributions identified in
[Attribution](ATTRIBUTION.md). Credit does not imply upstream endorsement or
coauthorship of this continuation.

The verification records describe computational checks performed within this
project. They are not external human peer review. The mathematical argument,
published certificate and executable checks are the basis for the result.
